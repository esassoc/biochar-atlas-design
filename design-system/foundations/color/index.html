Color

The ABI brand palette, extended for app use. The twelve issued colors are final; the ramps below are derived from them, and the shell is tinted on the palette's own Sage hue so it reads as the same family rather than a gray parked beside it.

Derived ramps

Twelve steps each, anchored so the issued hex appears unmodified at the step it actually occupies by lightness. That anchor moves per family, and where it lands tells you what the color is for: Green, Gold and Sage anchor at 9 — they are solid fills. Purple anchors at 12, a text and link color. Jade anchors at 5, a light tint.

Steps read the Radix way: 1–2 backgrounds, 3–5 surfaces, 6–7 borders, 8–10 solid fills, 11–12 text. Green earns its 9 twice over: Green Dark, the issued hover, lands on 10 by itself, and steps 11–12 extend past it so pressed states stay inside the scale.

Semantic roles

What components reference. The Biochar theme re-points these off the ramps above — each swatch below renders live from its token, so it reflects exactly what ships.

--color-primary #364500 ABI Green at green/9, the Radix home of a solid. Buttons, solid actions, focus rings, the active nav item. 10.46:1 on white, 8.83:1 on the sidenav.
--color-primary-hover #262903 Green Dark — the issued darker green, which falls out of the ramp at green/10 rather than being reached for.
--color-primary-subtle #e6eae0 Tinted fills on canvas and panel only (green/3). It disappears into the shell’s tinted chrome — ΔE 0.0126 against the sidenav — so it is never a state on chrome.
--color-primary-border #84906a Borders on primary-tinted surfaces (green/7). The first primary border in this palette to clear WCAG 1.4.11: 3.06 on canvas, 3.25 on the panel.
--color-secondary #676556 Sage at text weight (sage/11). Brand Sage itself fills; it never carries body copy.
--color-info #676556 Advisory messages — the quiet one. Sage family.
--color-success #177c52 Positive — suitable, saved, complete. Conventional green, deliberately not a brand color.
--color-warning #a45e00 Caution. Conventional amber at text/icon weight — the brand’s own Gold is 2.07:1 and cannot carry a state.
--color-danger #cd2a30 Negative. Conventional red. Status is functional convention; brand color carries identity.
--color-text-primary #21201c Body text. 15.59:1 on the panel, 12.90:1 on the topbar — the shell’s darkest surface.
--color-text-secondary #474540 Supporting text — brand Grey, doing real work.
--color-text-muted #615f5a Meta text. The palette floor: 5.05:1 on the topbar, and anything lighter fails AA there.
--color-text-link #45173d Purple on light, jade on dark — per the brand guide.
--color-border #d0cfcc Hairline dividers inside content (neutral/6). The shell’s own seams use bca-chrome-seam.
--color-surface #fafaf7 Cards, the workflow panel, and floating map controls. Near-white for table legibility, tinted just enough to hold an edge against the canvas (ΔE 0.0209).
--color-background #f4f3ee The canvas — report and landing layouts only. The suitability tool fills its content area edge to edge, so the canvas never paints there.
--color-surface-sunken #e8e5de Control wells, hover fills and status pills. Brand Ash exactly. No longer the sidebar — see the chrome tokens below.

App shell

Four surfaces on one hue, stepped in lightness, with chroma tapering as they lighten so the tint stays even instead of going chalky at the top. Listed darkest to lightest — the order they appear across the screen, left and top inward.

Two edges fall under the perceptual floor of ΔE 0.020 and are carried by the seam border rather than by fill: the sidenav against basemap land on map-bearing report layouts, and white over the panel. The panel cannot clear both the canvas and white — there is 0.036 of lightness between them and two visible steps need 0.040 — so it takes the canvas side, which is where cards actually sit.

--bca-chrome-topbar #e6e5dd Topbar. The darkest of the four by one tick — ΔE 0.0215 from the sidenav, just over the 0.020 floor. The midpoint of sage/4 and sage/5, so it is a rung of the ramp rather than a value picked to hit a number.
--bca-chrome-sidenav #edece5 App navigation. Sits ΔE 0.0427 from the workflow panel, down from 0.0783 when both this and the topbar were untinted.
--color-background #f4f3ee Canvas. ΔE 0.0218 from the sidenav.
--color-surface #fafaf7 Panel and cards. ΔE 0.0209 from the canvas.
--bca-chrome-seam #deddd4 The shell hairline. It carries the sidenav/basemap edge outright on map-bearing report layouts, where the two fills sit ΔE 0.0170 apart — under the perceptual floor.

Functional scales

The app's data-carrying color, kept separate from the brand ramps that feed the semantic chain. The suitability scale is the national map's five discrete SOH-BC classes — a Gold→Green path, which the ABI palette supplies natively. Every adjacent pair clears worst-case ΔE 0.120 under protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia; ColorBrewer's published YlGn-5 measures 0.092 on its own first pair under the same test. Class 1 carries chroma on purpose, because a pale achromatic light end collides with both the app chrome and the basemap; the freed achromatic value becomes "no data".

Labels never sit on classes 2–4 — best ink contrast against class 3 is 4.29:1. Values belong in the legend and the readout.

Single-purpose spoke tokens. Provenance matters here: NRCS users need to see at a glance whether a soil value came from SSURGO or from their own lab test — and since color is already spoken for by the rating classes, provenance separates by weight and by shape.

Theming

One [data-theme="biochar"] block in theme-biochar.css overrides the semantic layer; primitives never move. Links are the exception to chaining off primary — the brand guide puts them on Purple, so --color-text-link is set directly.

Gold cannot carry a state. White on Gold is 1.60:1 and white on Gold Dark 2.07:1, so Gold fills take dark labels only — Green Dark on Gold is 9.38:1, which is what the brand guide's own CTA examples already do. Gold Dark against the canvas is 1.90:1, under the 3.0 WCAG 1.4.11 requires of a non-text indicator, so focus rings, selected-state borders and icon-only controls come from Green or Purple.

Every solid fill takes a white label. A tone in the hub is three values — a fill, an ink for that fill, and a hover fill — and this theme moves its solids from step 9 to step 11, dark enough to carry white. Mapping only the fill left the hub's dark inks on our dark fills: secondary 2.06:1, success 2.11:1, warning 2.26:1. White clears every one — secondary and info 5.88, success 5.20, warning 5.02, danger 5.29, primary 10.46 — so no fill had to move.

Hovers stay in their family. Unmapped, four tones jumped to a hub color on hover — success to lime, warning to bright amber, info to blue. Each is now the OKLab midpoint of its own family's step 11 and step 12: success #1b5b3f, warning #78491c, danger #97212a, info #43412f. The midpoint rather than step 12 because esa-badge's dot variant fills from the hover token, and at step 12 an 8px dot stops reading as green, amber or red.

Focus rings are re-pointed. @esa/tokens defines --focus-ring-color twice, and the later one wins: component-tokens.css chains it to --color-grass-8 (#65ba74), a mint green from a ramp this brand doesn't use, measuring 2.18:1 against the canvas — below the 3.0 WCAG 1.4.11 requires. This theme points it back at Green, at 9.60:1.
